# Us2.ai

> FDA-cleared, fully automated AI echocardiography. Us2.ai analyzes a routine echo study and returns a complete structured report (measurements, disease detection, and findings) in seconds. Validated in 150+ peer-reviewed publications and used in 30+ countries. CE Class IIb, ISO 13485 / ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II, MDSAP, HIPAA, and GDPR compliant.

## Platform

- [Platform Overview](https://us2.ai/platform-overview/): How Us2.ai connects to ultrasound devices and PACS, processes studies in real time, and produces clinician-ready reports with zero clicks.

## Disease detection (Features)

- [Cardiac Amyloidosis](https://us2.ai/features/cardiac-amyloidosis/): First-and-only AI platform combining deep-learning pattern recognition with guideline-based echocardiographic measurements for automated detection. FDA-cleared and CE-marked.
- [Heart Failure](https://us2.ai/features/heart-failure/): Automated detection of heart failure phenotypes from a single routine echo, including diastolic dysfunction and reduced ejection fraction.
- [Aortic Stenosis](https://us2.ai/features/aortic-stenosis/): Automated severity grading and quantitative measurements aligned with international guidelines.
- [Pulmonary Hypertension](https://us2.ai/features/pulmonary-hypertension/): Right-heart-focused automated assessment including PASP, RV size and function.
- [Strain](https://us2.ai/features/strain/): Fully automated global longitudinal strain (GLS) and regional strain analysis.
- [Stress Echocardiography](https://us2.ai/features/stress-echocardiography/): Automated rest and stress wall-motion assessment for ischemia evaluation.
